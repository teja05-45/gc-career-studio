# GC Career Studio — Requirements Register

## Confirmed requirements

- A professional career-consultancy website with public service, contact, and success-story pages.
- Candidate registration and secure sign-in with role-based access for candidates, consultants, and administrators.
- Candidate-only discovery-call booking, backed by internal availability and PostgreSQL.
- Admin lead and booking visibility; lightweight consultant appointment visibility.
- Responsive, accessible UI using the warm off-white, forest-green visual system.

## Assumptions

- Discovery calls are managed with internal MVP availability; no external calendar is connected.
- Services, testimonials, and outcomes are editorial/demo content until GC Career Studio confirms production copy.
- A booking begins as `PENDING`; a staff member confirms it through a future workflow.
- Candidates own the booking journey; staff manage and fulfil bookings rather than creating candidate bookings from `/book`.

## Open questions

- Which audience, services, pricing, and conversion goal should be prioritised first?
- What calendar, CRM, email, and notification providers should be integrated?
- What are the confirmed service descriptions, consultant profiles, testimonials, and success metrics?
- Which candidate-profile, document, application, and career-plan capabilities belong in the next release?
